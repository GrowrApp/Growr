Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. Growr creates an anonymous account on first launch so your data can be backed up. Signing in with email or Google (Pro) lets you use your data on other devices and recover it after a reinstall.

Does Growr work offline?

Yes. Everything works without a connection and syncs when you are back online. Kill the app mid-workout and your draft is still there.

How do I export my data?

Settings → Data and backup → Export workouts (CSV). It is always free. You can also create a full backup file and restore it later.

Can I import my history from Strong?

Yes: Settings → Data and backup → Import from Strong. Exercise names that cannot be matched automatically appear on a review screen so you can pick the right exercise. Importing the same file again will not create duplicates.

The rest timer did not alert me with the screen off.

Make sure notifications are allowed for Growr and that exact alarms and battery optimisation are not blocking it (Android Settings → Apps → Growr). If it still fails, send a bug report with your phone model.

What happens to my data if Pro ends?

Nothing is deleted. You keep read access to all your data and can always export it. Only creating beyond the free limits and new cross-device sign-ins stop.

How do I restore a purchase or cancel a subscription?

Restore from the Pro screen in the app. Cancel in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app: Settings → Account and sync → Delete account and all data. Without the app, use the web deletion page.

What do you collect about me?

See the Privacy Policy. Usage statistics and crash reports are off unless you allow them.
