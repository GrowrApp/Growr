Account & Data Deletion Request

In accordance with Google Play Developer Policy, Growr provides users with full control to delete their account and all associated cloud data.

Method 1: In-App Immediate Deletion (Recommended)

If you have the Growr app installed on your device:

  1. Open the Growr app.
  2. Go to Settings (gear icon).
  3. Scroll to Account and select Delete Account & Data.
  4. Confirm deletion. Your account, local data, and cloud backups will be erased instantly.

Method 2: Web Deletion Request Form

If you have uninstalled the app or lost access to your device, submit a request below. We will process it within 30 days.

Data Retention Policy

Upon receiving a deletion request:

  • Your account credentials (email, UID, profile data) will be deleted from Firebase Authentication.
  • All associated Cloud Firestore backups (workouts, templates, exercises, body measurements, preferences) will be permanently purged.
  • Requests submitted via this form are processed within 30 days.
