Delete your account and data

You decide what happens to your data. There are two ways to delete your Growr account.

Option 1: in the app (instant)

  1. Open Growr and tap the gear icon for Settings.
  2. Open Account and sync.
  3. Tap Delete account and all data, type DELETE and confirm.

Your cloud data and account are removed and the app is reset on that device. You can export a CSV first from the same dialog. If asked, confirm your password or Google account again.

Option 2: request deletion here

Use this if you uninstalled the app or lost your phone. Enter the email address of the account you signed in with. If you only ever used the app anonymously, there is no email on file: tell us your phone model and roughly when you installed Growr, though we may not be able to identify an anonymous account without the device. We process requests within 30 days and email you when done.

What gets deleted

DataResult
Workouts, templates, programs, custom exercises, settings in the cloudPermanently deleted
Your account (email, sign-in identifier, user ID)Permanently deleted
Data stored on your phoneErased by in-app deletion; or uninstall the app
Usage statistics and crash reports (only if you allowed them)Not linked to your name or email; kept by Google under its standard retention, then deleted
Purchase recordsKept by Google Play and RevenueCat as required for billing and accounting law

Subscriptions are not cancelled by deleting your account. Cancel in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

Deletion cannot be undone. Want a copy first? Use Settings → Data → Export CSV in the app.
