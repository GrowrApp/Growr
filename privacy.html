Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026 · Applies to the Growr app for Android (com.growr.app) and this website.

The short version. Growr works offline. Your workouts are backed up to your own private cloud space so you can recover them. Usage statistics and crash reports are off until you choose to allow them. We do not show ads and we never sell your data.

1. What we collect

Age. Growr is for people aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you believe a child under 16 has used the app, email us and we will delete the data.

Workout data you create

Workouts, exercises, sets (weight, reps, RPE/RIR, set type, notes), templates, programs, custom exercises and your app settings. This is stored on your device and, when you are online, backed up to the cloud (see below).

Account data

The first time you open Growr it creates an anonymous account with a random user ID, with no name or email. If you choose to sign in with email or Google (a Pro feature, so you can use your data on other devices), we also receive your email address and, for Google, the name and sign-in identifier Google provides.

Subscription data

If you buy Pro, the purchase is handled by Google Play. Our subscription provider, RevenueCat, receives your random user ID and purchase and entitlement status so the app knows whether Pro is active. We never see your payment card details.

Usage statistics (only if you allow them)

Counts of a few milestones, linked to a random app instance ID (not your name or email): finishing onboarding, finishing a first workout, returning after 7 days, viewing the Pro screen and purchasing. These events contain no workout content. Google Analytics for Firebase may also receive standard device information such as app version, device model and operating system.

Crash reports (only if you allow them)

When the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics receives technical details such as the error, app version, device model and Android version, so the problem can be fixed.

Technical data needed to run the service

Firebase generates an installation identifier and checks that requests come from a genuine copy of the app (Firebase App Check with Google Play Integrity). The app also fetches settings such as free-plan limits from Firebase Remote Config. This happens regardless of your analytics choice because the app cannot work safely without it.

Messages you send us

If you email us feedback or a bug report from the app, your email app opens with the app version and device model filled in. We receive whatever you choose to send.

3. Your privacy choices

On first launch Growr asks whether to allow usage statistics and crash reports. Both are off until you decide. You can change either choice at any time in Settings → Privacy and legal. Turning a switch off stops further collection and deletes what is still waiting on your device (usage data and its app instance ID, or unsent crash reports). Data already collected is kept by Google in aggregated or limited form according to their retention settings.

4. Service providers

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, App Check, Remote Config, Analytics and Crashlytics when allowed). Google acts as our processor.
  • Google Play for app distribution and billing.
  • RevenueCat for subscription status.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website. Like any web host it receives your IP address when you visit.
  • Formspree receives the deletion requests sent with the form on the deletion page (the email address and anything you write).

These providers may process data outside the EU/EEA. Where they do, they rely on safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Workout data is kept as long as your account exists. If a Pro subscription ends, your data is kept and stays readable; only creating beyond the free limits and new sign-ins are restricted.
  • Delete in the app: Settings → Account and sync → Delete account and all data. This permanently removes your cloud data and your account, and erases the data on that device.
  • Delete from the web if you no longer have the app: use the deletion request page. We handle requests within 30 days.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it in Google Play.
  • Purchase records (your random user ID and purchase status) are kept by Google Play and RevenueCat as required for billing and accounting law, also after your account is deleted. They are no longer linked to your email or workout data. If you want the RevenueCat record removed as well, email us and we delete it by hand.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, object to processing, and withdraw consent at any time. Export is built in: Settings → Data and backup → Export workouts (CSV), always free. For anything else, email growrappli@gmail.com. You can also complain to your national data protection authority, for example Datatilsynet in Denmark.

7. Security

Data travels between the app and Firebase over HTTPS/TLS. Cloud data is protected by access rules so that only the signed-in owner can read or change their own data. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way we will update the date above and, where appropriate, tell you in the app.
